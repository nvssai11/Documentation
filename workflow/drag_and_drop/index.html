Drag-and-Drop Functionality

The drag-and-drop interface allows participants to move puzzle pieces interactively.

Client Actions

  1. A piece is dragged, triggering visual feedback.
  2. The piece is dropped onto a grid cell, signaling its placement.
  3. The client validates the piece's position for correctness.

Server Actions

  • Receives placement updates from clients.
  • Broadcasts the new state to all participants, ensuring synchronization.
